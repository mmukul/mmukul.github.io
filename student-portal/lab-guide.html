Assigned Local Lab

NextGen Local DevSecOps Lab

Your trainer has assigned a local lab. This environment runs on your own VM/host; the portal does not pretend that a public URL is the lab.

Setup

  1. Prepare your assigned Linux VM or host according to the training prerequisites.
  2. Use the lab repository/setup instructions supplied by your trainer.
  3. Run the approved setup script from the trainer-provided repository rather than an unverified URL.
  4. Return to the Student Portal after the environment is ready.

Security: Never paste Supabase keys, Turnstile secrets, payment credentials or other secrets into the lab setup page.
