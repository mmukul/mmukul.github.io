ACCOUNT & DATA CONTROL

Delete Account

Use this page to request deletion of your NextGen DevSecOps AI student account and associated personal data.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@nextgendevsecops.in.
  2. Use the subject line Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address used for your NextGen DevSecOps AI student account. You may include your name so we can identify the correct account.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are the account owner before processing the request.
We will use the information in your request only to identify and process the deletion request and to meet applicable security, legal and accounting requirements.

What is deleted

Subject to verification and applicable legal requirements, we will delete or de-identify account information and associated personal data that we no longer need to retain, which may include:

  • Student account/profile information
  • Contact information associated with the account
  • Learning and portal information associated with the account, where deletion is technically and legally appropriate
  • Other personal information that is not required for an applicable retention obligation

Information that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited or legally required period. This can include payment and transaction records, accounting/tax records, security and fraud-prevention records, dispute-related information, certificates or records needed to establish completed services, and information required by law.

Where retention is required, we will retain only what is reasonably necessary and protect it according to our applicable security practices.

Effect of account deletion

Deleting your account may remove access to the Student Portal, course resources, assignments, progress information or other account-based services associated with that account. Deletion does not automatically cancel a legal, financial or payment obligation that must be retained or resolved.

If you have an active course or pending service request, tell us in your deletion request if you want us to explain the impact before processing it.

Processing and support

After receiving a valid request, we will review and process it within a reasonable period, subject to identity verification, technical constraints and applicable legal or security requirements.
