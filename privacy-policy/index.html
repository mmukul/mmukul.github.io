PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NextGen DevSecOps AI, operated by Mukul Malhotra, handles information in connection with the website, training services, student portal and NextGen DevSecOps AI mobile application.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

1. Who we are

NextGen DevSecOps AI provides technology training, consulting, learning resources and related student services. The website is nextgendevsecops.in.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@nextgendevsecops.in.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use our website, student portal, forms or mobile application, we may collect:

  • Personal and contact information: name, email address and phone/mobile number.
  • Account information: student account identifiers and information needed to authenticate and provide student-portal functionality.
  • Training and enrollment information: selected course, enrollment status, learning or assignment information and related service requests.
  • Payment and transaction information: course/payment plan, transaction reference and payment status. Payment credentials such as card PIN, UPI PIN and OTP are handled by the payment provider and are not intentionally stored by NextGen DevSecOps AI.
  • Enquiry information: information you provide when requesting a demo, training, consulting, mentoring, interview/project preparation or other support.
  • Security and technical information: limited information used to protect forms and services from abuse, including information processed by Cloudflare Turnstile and normal web/server security logs.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and operate the website, mobile application and student portal.
  • Create and manage student accounts and provide requested learning services.
  • Process course enrollment, payments and payment verification.
  • Respond to demo, training, consulting and other enquiries.
  • Provide learning resources, assignments, progress and certificates where applicable.
  • Protect forms, accounts and services against fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability and security of our services.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and compliance requirements.

4. Service providers

We use selected technology providers to operate our services. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

  • Supabase: authentication, database and secure backend services.
  • Razorpay: payment processing and transaction services for supported payment methods.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: security verification and abuse prevention for protected forms and login-related flows.
  • GitHub Pages / related hosting infrastructure: website hosting and delivery where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Payments

Where online payment is offered, payment processing is performed through Razorpay or another payment provider identified at checkout. Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies.

NextGen DevSecOps AI does not intentionally request or store your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or one-time password. Do not share such credentials with the trainer or support team.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar mechanisms required for security, authentication, session handling and normal operation. Third-party security or payment components may also use their own technologies when you interact with them.

We do not use the website primarily to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the services we provide. Data transmitted between your device and supported services is generally protected using HTTPS/TLS. Access to backend data is controlled through authentication, authorization and database security controls where applicable.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a secure device, protect your account credentials and avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, PINs or other authentication secrets.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain legitimate business and training records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, meet legal/accounting requirements and maintain security. Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting support@nextgendevsecops.in with the subject Account and Data Deletion Request.

We may need to verify the request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate financial/accounting records, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other lawful purposes.

If you have an active course, payment, certificate or other service record, deletion may affect access to that service. We will explain any applicable limitation when processing the request.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults, students and technology professionals who are able to use the services with appropriate consent and authorization. We do not knowingly target children with advertising or knowingly collect children's personal information for unrelated purposes.

11. Third-party links and services

Our website and application may link to third-party services such as YouTube, GitHub, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Razorpay or other external resources. When you leave our services or interact directly with a third party, that third party's privacy policy and terms apply to its processing of your information.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal requirements or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
