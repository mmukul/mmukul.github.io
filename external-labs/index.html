INDEPENDENT CLOUD LAB SERVICE

External Labs

A standalone lab platform for external organizations and users. It does not use NextGen training accounts, course enrolments or student records.

Service configuration required

Connect this portal to its dedicated Supabase project by creating config.js from config.js.example, applying supabase/schema.sql to that project, and deploying this folder independently.

The portal will remain unavailable until its independent identity and database are configured.
